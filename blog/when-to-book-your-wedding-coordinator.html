When Should You Book Your Wedding Coordinator?

One of the most common questions we get is simply: when should I actually book this?

Day-of Coordination — book 1 to 3 months out

Because DOC starts around 4 weeks before the wedding, you can technically book relatively close to the date — but earlier is safer, especially during peak wedding season when availability fills up.

Partial Planning — book 6 to 9 months out

Since Partial Planning starts around 6 months before the wedding, booking with that runway gives enough time to properly finish sourcing vendors and nail the details without feeling rushed.

Full Wedding Planning — book as early as possible

Full planning benefits most from lead time — ideally 9 to 18 months out. The earlier you start, the more choice you have with vendors and venues, and the more the budget can be built thoughtfully rather than reactively.

The general rule

If you're unsure whether you need support at all, book a free chat as soon as the thought crosses your mind. There's no downside to talking early, and it means whichever service fits is still available for your date.
