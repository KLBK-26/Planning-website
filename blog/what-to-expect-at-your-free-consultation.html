What to Expect at Your Free Wedding Planning Consultation

A free consultation is a low-pressure way to figure out what kind of support actually fits your wedding — here's how it works.

Before the call

When you book, you'll answer a couple of quick questions — your wedding date, and roughly what you're after. That's it. No lengthy form, no obligation.

During the call

We'll talk through where you're at with planning, what's already booked, and what you're hoping the day feels like. This is also your chance to ask anything — about pricing, process, availability, whatever's on your mind.

What we'll figure out together

By the end of the call, you should have a clear sense of whether Day-of Coordination, Partial Planning, or Full Planning actually fits — and if you're still not sure, that's completely fine too.

After the call

There's no pressure to decide on the spot. If it feels right, we'll walk through booking; if not, you'll still walk away with clarity on what your day actually needs.

Book your free chat — it takes less time than you'd think.
