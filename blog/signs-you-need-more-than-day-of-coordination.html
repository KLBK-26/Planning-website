5 Signs You Need More Than Day-of Coordination

Day-of Coordination is perfect for a lot of couples — but not all. Here's how to tell if you actually need more support.

1. You haven't booked most of your vendors yet

DOC assumes the big decisions are already made. If your venue, caterer, and celebrant aren't locked in, you likely need Partial or Full Planning instead.

2. You're feeling overwhelmed by the number of decisions

If every planning conversation feels like a fresh source of stress, more hands-on support earlier in the process will help far more than a coordinator who only appears in the final weeks.

3. You don't have time to chase vendors

Sourcing quotes, comparing options, and confirming details takes real hours. If that's not realistic around work and life, Partial Planning takes that off your plate.

4. You want design and styling guidance, not just execution

DOC oversees setup on the day but doesn't build your vision from scratch. If you want help shaping the overall look and feel, that's a Full Planning conversation.

5. Your wedding date is still a long way off

If you're 12+ months out and haven't started, that's actually the ideal window for Full Planning — you get the most value from a planner the earlier they're involved.
