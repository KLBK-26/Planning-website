Founder

The part I loved most, before it was even the job.

I didn't plan to become a wedding coordinator — I became one on the job. Working weddings, I kept finding myself stepping in: fixing timelines, wrangling vendors, calming down a stressed bridal party. Somewhere along the way I realised that was the part I loved most.

Now, planning my own wedding, I'm living the exact process I help couples through — so I know firsthand what it feels like from the other side too.

I'm not here to hand you a checklist. I'm here to keep your day stress-free and make sure you actually get to enjoy every part of it, the way you pictured it.
