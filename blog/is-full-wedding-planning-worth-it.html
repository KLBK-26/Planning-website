Is Full Wedding Planning Worth It?

Full Wedding Planning is the most comprehensive — and most expensive — of the three services. So is it actually worth it?

What you're really paying for

It's not just the wedding day. It's every hour spent sourcing vendors, comparing quotes, building the budget, and making sure the whole thing comes together as one cohesive vision, rather than a collection of separate bookings.

Where it saves you time

Couples consistently underestimate how many hours wedding planning actually takes — research suggests dozens of hours across research, meetings, and admin. Full planning takes almost all of that off your plate.

Where it saves you money

A planner who knows the local vendor landscape can often help you avoid costly mistakes, negotiate better, and prioritise spend where it actually shows — offsetting some of the planning cost itself.

Who it's genuinely worth it for

If you're time-poor, want a proper design vision, or simply don't want to spend the next year deep in spreadsheets, Full Planning tends to be worth every dollar. If you enjoy the planning process and just want a hand finishing it, Partial Planning might be the better fit.
