What is Day-of Coordination? A Guide for NSW Couples

You've planned everything. The venue is booked, the flowers are ordered, the dress is hanging up. But on the morning of your wedding, who's actually running the day?

That's where day-of coordination comes in — and it's one of the most underrated decisions a couple can make.

So, what does a day-of coordinator actually do?

A day-of coordinator steps in to manage everything on your wedding day so you don't have to. They're the person your vendors call when they have a question. They're the one who knows your timeline inside out. They handle the bump in the road you don't even know is coming — and you never find out about it because it's already sorted.

At Kapturing Love by K, day-of coordination includes preparation well before the wedding itself. That means reviewing your plans, confirming details with your suppliers, building a clear timeline, and being fully across every moving part before we even arrive on the day.

What it's not

Day-of coordination isn't just someone turning up and holding a clipboard. It's calm, organised support from someone who genuinely cares how your day feels — not just how it looks on a run sheet.

It's also not the same as full wedding planning. Day-of coordination suits couples who have done most of the planning themselves but want a calm, experienced presence to bring it all together on the day.

Is it right for you?

If you've ever caught yourself thinking "I just want to enjoy my own wedding" — day-of coordination is probably right for you. It's especially valuable for regional and country weddings across NSW, where logistics are more complex and vendor communication can be harder to manage solo.
