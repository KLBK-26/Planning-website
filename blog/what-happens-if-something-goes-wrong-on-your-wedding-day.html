What Happens If Something Goes Wrong on Your Wedding Day?

Almost every wedding has at least one small thing go sideways. The difference is whether you find out about it.

The kind of things that come up

A vendor running late, weather changing plans, a missing detail, a timeline slipping by twenty minutes. Individually small — but without someone managing them, they compound.

Why a coordinator changes the outcome

With a dedicated point of contact running the day, these issues get solved quietly and quickly, without pulling you, your partner, or your family into problem-solving mode on your own wedding day.

The emergency kit

A well-stocked kit covers everything from a sewing emergency to a headache to a scuffed shoe — small fixes that keep the day moving without a scramble.

The goal

You should be able to look back on your wedding and remember how it felt — not the moment a vendor rang the wrong number. That's exactly what Day-of Coordination is there to absorb.
