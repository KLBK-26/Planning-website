Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Who we are

This website is operated by K&BD PTY LTD trading as Kapturing Love by K (ABN available on request). We offer wedding photography, videography, content creation, and planning and coordination services across New South Wales, Australia.

We are committed to protecting your privacy in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

What information we collect

We collect personal information when you complete an enquiry form on this website. This may include:

  • Your name and your partner's name
  • Email address and phone number
  • Wedding date, venue, and location
  • Guest count estimate and budget range
  • The services you are interested in
  • Any additional details you share in a message field

A popup may appear when you go to leave the site, inviting you to stay in touch. If you choose to submit this form, we collect your name, email address, and phone number to follow up about your enquiry. A link to book a free consultation is also provided, which opens an external booking tool (Google Calendar).

How we collect your information

All forms on this website are processed by Netlify (Netlify, Inc.), a third-party form handling and hosting provider. When you submit a form, your information is transmitted securely to Netlify and forwarded to us by email. Netlify's privacy policy is available at netlify.com/privacy.

Session recording and analytics — Microsoft Clarity

This website uses Microsoft Clarity, a behaviour analytics tool provided by Microsoft Corporation. Clarity helps us understand how visitors interact with our website by recording anonymised session data, including mouse movements, clicks, and scrolling behaviour.

Clarity may collect the following information:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Mouse movements, clicks, and scroll depth
  • Session recordings of how you interact with the page
  • Device type, browser, and general location (country or region level)

Clarity automatically masks sensitive form fields such as passwords and payment inputs. We have configured Clarity to mask all text input fields on our enquiry forms so that personal details you type are not recorded.

Clarity uses cookies to identify returning sessions. These cookies do not identify you personally. Microsoft's privacy policy is available at privacy.microsoft.com.

A notice is displayed on your first visit informing you that session recording is in use. You can decline by clicking "Decline" in the notice, which will prevent Clarity from loading during your visit.

Cookies and local storage

This website uses the following browser storage:

  • Microsoft Clarity cookies — used for session analytics as described above. These persist across visits.
  • Session storage (klbk_popup_shown) — used to remember whether you have already seen our exit popup during a single visit. This is deleted automatically when you close your browser tab and is never sent to us.
  • Local storage (klbk_cookie_notice) — used to remember that you have dismissed the cookie notice so it does not appear on every visit.

We do not use advertising cookies or share cookie data with any advertising networks.

Why we collect your information

We collect your personal information solely to:

  • Respond to your wedding enquiry
  • Provide information about our services and availability
  • Follow up if you have requested contact
  • Understand how our website is being used so we can improve the experience for couples

We will not use your information for any other purpose without your consent.

Who we share your information with

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information is shared only with:

  • Netlify — as our form processing and hosting provider
  • Microsoft Clarity — anonymised session analytics only, as described above

We may disclose your information if required to do so by law or a court order.

How long we keep your information

Enquiry information is retained only as long as necessary to respond to your enquiry and conduct normal business operations. If you become a client, relevant information will be retained for the duration of your booking and for a reasonable period afterwards for business record-keeping purposes.

Unsolicited contact

We only contact people who have voluntarily submitted an enquiry form or left their details via our popup. We do not send marketing emails to people who have not given us their contact details directly and explicitly.

Your rights

Under the Australian Privacy Act, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information (where we are not legally required to retain it)
  • Make a complaint if you believe your privacy has been breached

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

Complaints

If you have a privacy concern or complaint, please contact us in the first instance. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) at oaic.gov.au.

Changes to this policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated date.
